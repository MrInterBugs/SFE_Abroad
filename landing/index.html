Independent repayment tools for UK graduates living abroad

Aedan L publishes practical calculators and guides for UK student loan borrowers overseas. The main tool helps graduates estimate monthly Student Loans Company repayments using overseas threshold tables, repayment plan rules, country-specific exchange rates, and gross local salary.

Open the repayment calculator Read the methodology

What the calculator covers

The calculator supports Plan 1, Plan 2, Plan 4, Plan 5, and Postgraduate Loan estimates for supported tax years. It converts a local gross salary into GBP using the published overseas threshold table, compares that income with the relevant country threshold, and estimates the monthly repayment due above the threshold.

  • Country-specific overseas thresholds and exchange rates.
  • Separate undergraduate and Postgraduate Loan repayment rates.
  • Worked country examples and plan-specific guidance.
  • Clear caveats so users can compare estimates with official SLC notices.
